# Startup payroll customer discovery kit

A kit for learning how Indian startups with 10–150 staff run payroll and statutory compliance, through 10–12 interviews with founders, HR leads and finance leads. It contains the research plan, interview guide, outreach messages, note-taking and synthesis templates, and desk research with sources.

*Independent student research project, not affiliated with any company named.*

## Why this exists

Payroll software for Indian startups is crowded, yet little public evidence shows how small teams actually run payroll, what goes wrong, and what makes them change. Opinions are cheap; a walk-through of last month's payroll run is not. This kit is built to collect that evidence honestly: falsifiable hypotheses set before any interview, questions about past behaviour rather than hypotheticals, and a synthesis method that reports counts, not inflated percentages.

## How to use

Run the kit in this order. Times are rough estimates.

| Step | What | Time |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | Read [05](05_desk_research.md) and [01](01_research_plan.md). Check the Labour Codes status and prices are still current. Adjust hypotheses if needed. | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Pilot [02](02_interview_guide.md) with 1–2 friendly contacts. Cut questions that confuse. | 2 hours |
| 3 | Recruit with [03](03_screener_and_outreach.md). Log every message and reply. | 6–8 hours over 2–3 weeks |
| 4 | Run interviews. Fill a copy of the notes template within 24 hours of each. | 1 hour each, about 12 hours |
| 5 | Code each interview into [synthesis_tracker.csv](synthesis_tracker.csv). Delete the EXAMPLE row first. | 15 minutes each, about 3 hours |
| 6 | Affinity-map, score the hypotheses, and write up with [04](04_synthesis_report_template.md). | 6–8 hours |

Total: about 30–35 hours over 4–6 weeks.

Keep raw notes and recordings out of this repository. Commit only anonymised, coded data.

## Sources

Full citations with access dates are in [05_desk_research.md](05_desk_research.md#sources). Key sources:

- Rob Fitzpatrick, [The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/): interview method.
- Guest, Bunce and Johnson (2006), [How Many Interviews Are Enough?](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1525822x05279903), *Field Methods* 18(1): sample size.
- Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, [A Guide to the New Labour Codes](https://www.cyrilshroff.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Guide-to-the-Labour-Codes.pdf), and [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-127.html): Labour Codes.
- ESIC [coverage](https://esic.gov.in/coverage) and [contribution](https://esic.gov.in/contribution) pages: ESI.
- Vendor pricing pages and Capterra review pages: software options and review themes.
