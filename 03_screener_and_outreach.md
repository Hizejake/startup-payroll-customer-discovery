# Screener and outreach

**Summary.** Five screener questions to confirm fit before booking a call, and three outreach messages: LinkedIn, email and WhatsApp. The messages say plainly that I am a student doing research and am not selling anything. Send the screener as a short form or ask it in the first reply. Criteria come from [01_research_plan.md](01_research_plan.md).

## Screener

| # | Question | Accept | Screen out |
|---|----------|--------|------------|
| S1 | Roughly how many people are on your company's payroll today? (10–24 / 25–49 / 50–150 / other) | 10–150 | Under 10 or over 150 |
| S2 | In the last 3 months, what was your role in payroll? (I prepare it / I check or approve it / I'm not involved) | Prepare, or check or approve | Not involved |
| S3 | How is payroll run today? (Spreadsheet / Payroll or HR software / A CA or outsourced firm / A mix) | Any | — (used to balance the sample) |
| S4 | Where is the company based, and what does it do? | India-based | Not based in India |
| S5 | Do you work for, or advise, a payroll software, HR software or CA firm? | No | Yes |

Use S1 and S3 to keep the size bands and methods balanced. Stop booking a band once it has 4 interviews.

## Outreach messages

Replace everything in [brackets]. Send one follow-up after 5–7 days, then stop.

### 1. LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters)

> Hi [Name], I'm a final-year student at Jadavpur University researching how startups run payroll and compliance. Could I hear about your experience on a 30-minute call? I'm not selling anything, and I'll share the anonymised findings.

*(233 characters including the placeholder, leaving room for a long name.)*

### 2. Email

**Subject:** Student research: how [Company] runs payroll (30 minutes)

> Dear [Name],
>
> I'm Arpan Mukherjee, a final-year student at Jadavpur University. I'm researching how Indian startups with 10–150 staff run payroll and compliance: who does the work, what goes wrong, and how teams choose tools.
>
> [I found you through [source].] Could you spare 30 minutes for a call in the next two weeks? I would ask about how your last payroll run actually happened. I'm not selling anything, and I won't name you or [Company] anywhere.
>
> In return, I'll send you the anonymised summary of what 10–12 startups told me.
>
> If a call isn't possible, a pointer to whoever runs payroll at [Company] would help just as much.
>
> Thank you,
> Arpan Mukherjee
> [Phone] · [LinkedIn URL]

### 3. WhatsApp (after a warm introduction)

> Hi [Name], I'm Arpan. [Introducer] shared your number. I'm a final-year JU student researching how startups run payroll and compliance. Could we do a 30-minute call this week or next? No selling, and your company stays anonymous. Happy to work around your schedule.

### Follow-up (any channel)

> Hi [Name], a gentle follow-up on my note about payroll research. If now isn't a good time, no problem at all. If someone else at [Company] handles payroll, an introduction would help a lot. Thank you.
