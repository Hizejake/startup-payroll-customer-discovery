## Coding rules

- **pain_score_1to5:** my rating after the call, not theirs. 1 = no workaround, no complaint. 3 = workaround in place, occasional cost. 5 = money or trust already lost, and they are actively looking for a fix.
- **hours_per_month:** total for everyone inside the company, last month. Excludes the CA.
- **errors_last_6_months:** wrong or late salaries, and late or wrong PF, ESI, PT or TDS payments or filings.
- **decision_maker:** who approved the last change of payroll method or tool. Use "none_yet" if there was never a change.
